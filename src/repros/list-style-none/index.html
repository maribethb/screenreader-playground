Safari drops list semantics when the bullets are hidden

If you hide a list's bullets with list-style: none, Safari stops telling VoiceOver it's a list. Adding role="list" back to the list fixes it.

Affected

Steps to reproduce

  1. Navigate to the lists under the "Repro" heading. The first list has bullets, and the second doesn't.
  2. Navigate through the items of both lists one at a time (VO+Right Arrow in VoiceOver, Down Arrow in NVDA and JAWS).
  3. Repeat with the other variant.

Expected output

Both lists are announced as lists of 3 items, and each item is read with its position, like "1 of 3". Hiding the bullets is a visual change and shouldn't change what the screenreader says.

Actual output

With VoiceOver in Safari, the first list is announced as a list, but the second isn't, and its items are read without their position. With role="list", both lists are announced the same way.

Variants

  • list-style-none: the second list has list-style: none. This is the broken version.
  • role-list: the same, with role="list" on the second list. This is the workaround.
